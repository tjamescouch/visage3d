# visage3d

3D avatar surface for LLM-driven characters.

This repository hosts the 3D/web viewer layer (rendering, camera, lighting, animation playback) and integration points for persona-driven behavior.

## components

- [web](components/web.md) - browser-based 3D viewer surface
- [assets](components/assets.md) - avatar assets acquisition, caching, and attribution
